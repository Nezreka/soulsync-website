About SoulSync

SoulSync is an automated music discovery and collection management platform built for people who run their own media servers.

If you've wrestled with Lidarr — the broken imports, the confusing profiles, the indexers that never seem to work right — SoulSync was built out of that exact frustration.

It replaces the entire Lidarr ecosystem with a single application that actually works out of the box. No quality profiles. No root folder headaches. No wondering why half your library won't match.

SoulSync monitors your artists, discovers new music based on your taste, automatically downloads and organizes tracks, and generates smart playlists — all without you lifting a finger.

Built with Python and Flask, designed for self-hosters, and open source forever.
